augustus81 uploader

Google Cloud project: augustus81-yt

What this app does

augustus81 uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner, August, to upload videos to and manage his own YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@augustus81.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and no other user's account is accessed.

It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and to set the title, description, thumbnail and publishing schedule of videos on the owner's own channel.
